== intro

Our task was to bring to life Gaku Keito’s manga prints for the 14th episode of the NBA Beat series.

We fully immersed ourselves in the Gaku Keito universe to craft bold animations that stayed true to the artist’s vision. The creative direction of these sequences elevated the final video production, which enjoyed great success across the web.
