== intro

Our task was to bring to life the graphic design for Japan Airlines’ ‘Fly to Hokkaido’ campaign.

The brief called for restraint, so we built the motion around clarity. Clean transitions, legible typography, and a rhythm that lets the destination do the talking — everything designed to guide the viewer rather than compete with the image.
