== intro

Our task was to edit and craft motion graphics and kinetic typography for Dialogue.

For a corporate project like this, we aimed for a subtle approach in motion graphics and kinetic typography. We created something smooth yet high-end, incorporating 3D animation to convey information clearly to the viewers.
