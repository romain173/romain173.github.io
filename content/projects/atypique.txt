== intro

Our task was to create an entire commercial campaign for Atypique mocktails from scratch.

Driven by mixed-media techniques, we worked closely with the director to craft a bright, colorful universe for Atypique — the most cocktail of mocktails. Each flavor ad followed the same creative approach — combining live-action footage with energetic editing.
