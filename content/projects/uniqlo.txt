== intro

Our task was to bring 16 graphic t-shirt to life for Uniqlo’s ‘UT’ global campaign.Working closely with the director, we used various forms of dynamic motion design and kinetic typography to give this video an eye-catching style, capturing customers’ attention in stores worldwide.
