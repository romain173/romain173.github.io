== intro

Cinco is a marketing agency that has been creating measurable campaigns for leading global brands through physical and digital activations for the past 20 years.

Today, the company is pushing industry boundaries by integrating conversational AI and immersive environments by way of its innovative *AI Experience Room* platform.

## Our Strategic & Creative Approach

### 01 — **Mixed Media Strategy**
To fully capture the innovative nature of the *AI Experience Room *platform, a mixed-media strategy was the ideal creative vehicle to convey abstract technological concepts through high-end visuals.

### 04 — **Optimized Production **
Due to the short deadline, the project combined two parallel production lines: one dedicated to design and motion, the other to AI asset generation, which were merged in post-production.
This dual-track workflow maximized efficiency and enabled the delivery, on schedule, of flawless assets.
