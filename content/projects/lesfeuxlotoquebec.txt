== intro

Our task was to create a commercial campaign for the international Grands Feux de Loto-Québec, entirely from scratch.

Our goal was to make viewers feel like they were right there, at the show, so we created a dynamic, elegant universe. Through mixed-media techniques and 3D kinetic typography, we brought the fireworks experience to life for future audiences!
