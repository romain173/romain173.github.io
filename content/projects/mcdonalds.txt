== intro

Our task was to develop the concept, craft a director’s treatment, and edit the commercial for Kerwin Frost’s McNugget Buddies.

Working closely with the director, we built a concept allowing us to disclose something we couldn’t actually show just yet… Kerwin Frost’s McNugget Buddies. To go above and beyond, we gave the entire piece a bold, 90s-inspired aesthetic treatment and delivered a dynamic edit designed to grab the viewer’s attention.
