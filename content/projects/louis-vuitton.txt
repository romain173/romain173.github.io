== intro

Our task was to craft typography and logo animation for the Louis Vuitton x J-Hope Visionary Journeys campaign.

The request was to create static text, and while we proposed more experimental concepts, Louis Vuitton, true to their classic style, opted for the simplest possible typography—But it didn’t matter to us, because working on a Louis Vuitton project with J-Hope was already a proud moment!
