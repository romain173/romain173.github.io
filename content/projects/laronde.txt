== intro

Our task was to create a commercial campaign for the Six Flags amusement park, La Ronde, entirely from scratch.

We wanted this commercial to feel like a rollercoaster ride, so we designed a thrilling, adrenaline-charged universe. Through mixed-media techniques and what we called ‘colorful chaos,’ we gave future customers a taste of the La Ronde experience!
