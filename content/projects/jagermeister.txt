== intro

Our task was to develop a director’s treatment and craft a motion system for Jägermeister’s Around the Block global campaign.

We were the creative team behind the first four episodes in Tokyo and we set the visual tone for the whole campaign. To match Jägermeister’s vibrant nightlife world, we designed a bold and colorful motion graphics system, which we then turned into a flexible toolkit for directors all around the world.
